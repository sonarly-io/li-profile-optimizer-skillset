# Ethics And Boundaries

LinkedIn profile analysis often involves personal data. Keep the work limited to profile communication, positioning, and public professional presentation.

## Data Handling

- Prefer user-provided screenshots, profile text, resumes, job descriptions, or target-audience notes.
- If the user asks to browse a public profile, access only what is publicly available and cite sources.
- Do not bypass access controls, scrape at scale, use fake accounts, or advise on evading platform limits.
- Do not store, expose, or republish profile data beyond the user's requested deliverable.

## Sensitive Inferences

Do not infer, score, or discuss protected or sensitive traits unless the user supplied them as explicit, relevant self-descriptions and asks to incorporate them into public-facing copy.

Avoid inferences about:
- Age, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, political beliefs, disability, health, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity, family status, or socioeconomic background.
- Personality, honesty, mental state, or cultural fit from photos, names, schools, gaps, or writing style.

## High-Impact Decisions

Do not make or automate decisions about hiring, firing, compensation, credit, insurance, housing, education, legal status, or eligibility. If the user is evaluating a candidate, frame the output as profile communication observations and missing evidence, not a decision recommendation.

Allowed:
- "The profile does not visibly show Python production experience."
- "The About section would be stronger with evidence of team scope."

Avoid:
- "Do not hire this person."
- "This candidate is unreliable."
- "They are probably too old for this role."

## Accuracy And Representation

- Do not fabricate credentials, employers, degrees, metrics, recommendations, awards, or endorsements.
- Mark unverified claims with placeholders.
- Encourage the user to confirm numbers, dates, titles, and claims before publishing.
- Preserve the user's truthful experience even when rewriting for stronger positioning.

## Competitive And Third-Party Analysis

When comparing multiple profiles:
- Compare observable profile strategy, not personal worth.
- Avoid ranking people on sensitive traits or hidden assumptions.
- Focus on what each profile communicates to the target audience.
